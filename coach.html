How the Coach works

Two modes, automatic. Leave Opt entry empty = SCOUTING: you are flat, and every briefing hunts the entry — verdict reads WAIT (no trigger yet) or ENTER (trigger met), with the exact order to place. Fill Opt entry = IN TRADE: she manages the position — HOLD / TIGHTEN / TRIM / EXIT.

The sections. SITUATION = what the tape is doing right now, in numbers. VERDICT = the one-word decision. WHY = three reasons tied to your P/L. LEVELS = next price above and below that matter. ORDERS = the exact ticket to have resting in Fidelity, dollar-precise — the part you act on. LESSON = the concept in play. NEXT = the tripwire before the next 15-minute pass.

Voice. Reads the full briefing slowly, section by section, then repeats the verdict and orders. Won't interrupt while you type. Every alert lands in the log below for replay.

The law still rules. She is decision support, not authority: your resting bracket, the Eleven Gates, lunch window, 2:45 flat, and 21 DTE outrank any verdict. If she says HOLD and your stop says out — you are out.

Feed her honestly. Fills line (4@8.90, 1@8.35 → AVG), real stop and target in stock terms, chain loaded. Stale inputs = confident coaching of a ghost position.
