KORNIS PROTOCOL — TOMORROW SHEET
I · The close & the nightrefresh
Build the sheet after the 3:00 close. It reads the day, then writes tomorrow's plan for every name on the board.
II · Tomorrow's plan — every name, both sides
III · The morning script
The night before (after this sheet builds): read the lean and the two triggers for each name. Decide nothing. Print it.
7:45 CT — London refresh: the sheet repriced. Names whose pre-market print has already gapped through a trigger are flagged — those triggers are dead; the 8:35 range replaces them.
8:15 — the Desk: open it. The Desk carries this sheet's lean and levels; the live score is the verdict, the sheet is the argument.
8:30–8:35 — hands off. The range prints. Then: 5-minute CLOSE beyond the trigger WITH volume. Bracket on the fill. Half at T1, stop to breakeven. Flat by 2:45. One name a day.
