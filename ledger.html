The Ledger — Your Personal Backtest

EVERY TRADE TEACHES THE MACHINE WHAT WORKS FOR *YOU*
Why this page exists: every subscription service sells you signals backtested on everyone. Nobody can sell you the only statistic that matters: which signals are green when you win and which are green when you lose. Log every closed trade here — after ~20, the numbers stop being noise and start being your edge, measured.

Expectancy = what one average trade pays you. (Win% × avg win) − (Loss% × avg loss). Positive = the process makes money even with losers; negative = size and stops need work before anything else. This single number outranks win rate: a 40% win rate with 3:1 winners beats a 70% win rate with 1:3 losers.
3 · What Actually Pays You — signal by signal

How to read this: "Edge" = your win rate when the signal was green MINUS when it was red. A signal with +30 edge is load-bearing for YOU — never trade without it. A signal near 0 is decoration in your hands. Sample-size law: ignore any row with n under 10 — small samples lie confidently. That's why the machine won't recalibrate until it has real data.
