Latest Research Playbook — 2024–2026 Literature

Distilled from current academic papers (SSRN, arXiv, J. Futures Markets, J. Financial Econometrics), exchange research (CBOE), and institutional desk practice. Paraphrased findings — verify anything before you trade it.
1 · Dealer Gamma Is the New Market Structure
The academic consensus (Eraker/Vilkov/Dim SSRN; CBOE gamma-squeeze research) is that market-maker net gamma measurably shapes intraday behavior: positive dealer gamma → mean reversion and pinning (dealers buy dips/sell rips); negative dealer gamma → momentum and cascades (dealers sell weakness/buy strength). CBOE's own research quantified the effect at up to ~3 percentage points of annualized realized vol. Practical rule: identify the gamma-flip level, call wall, and put wall each session. Fade moves toward walls in positive-gamma regimes; ride breaks in negative-gamma regimes. Exogenous catalysts (FOMC, CPI, news) can shatter the walls — regime awareness beats levels.
SSRN 4692190 · cboe.com gamma research · SpotGamma/MenthorQ practice
2 · The 0DTE Regime Changed Everything
0DTE now runs roughly half of SPX volume — a structural shift, not a fad. Key findings: MM 0DTE inventory gamma is on average positive (stabilizing), but when it flips negative, intraday momentum strengthens. Institutional desks harvest premium via hedged intraday strangles; directional traders time entries when IV is cheap vs the expected intraday move. For your long-only style: your Aug-14 expiry position was a 0DTE trade whether you called it that or not — the discipline for those is time-stops (out if no movement in 30 min), regime-stops (out if gamma flips), and hard scaling rules, because ±100% swings in minutes are normal.
SSRN 4692190 · Resonanz Capital institutional review · CBOE volume data
3 · Machine Learning Beats Closed-Form on Vol Forecasting
The 2025–2026 journal literature converges on hybrid models: parametric option-pricing model first (Black-Scholes/Heston), then a neural network trained on the residual errors. These "theory-guided ML" hybrids beat both pure models and pure ML on out-of-sample IV forecasting and delta-neutral trading Sharpe. LSTMs win at short maturities; GARCH-family still competitive at long maturities. Retail translation: you can't run these live, but the finding that short-dated IV is predictable from recent surface dynamics justifies watching IV term-structure changes day-over-day as an edge signal — rising front-end IV vs stable back-end = the market pricing an imminent move.
J. Futures Markets 2026 (Duan; Jiang) · J. Financial Econometrics · arXiv rough-vol literature
4 · Regime Classification Before Direction
New ETF research frames the daily problem as binary classification — "trending day" vs "oscillating day" — before any directional call, because strategy selection depends on it: oscillating days favor mean-reversion (fade band extremes), trending days favor momentum (buy breaks, no fading). Your dashboard's regime dial is a manual version of this. The literature's inputs: overnight gap size, opening-range width, VIX level/term structure, and prior-day realized vol. Rule of thumb from the research: the first 30–60 minutes usually reveals the day type — trade small or not at all before classification.
MDPI JRFM 2026 · intraday commonality literature
5 · Option Momentum & Return Predictability
Heston et al. (J. Finance 2023) documented option momentum — strategies that performed recently tend to persist — and Bali et al. (RFS 2023) showed ML on big options data predicts option returns cross-sectionally. The stable retail-usable factors: expensive IV relative to subsequent realized vol loses money for buyers persistently (the variance risk premium), so buying options systematically only wins with a timing edge; and liquidity (tight spreads, high OI) is a return factor in itself — your screener's spread% flag is the direct application.
J. Finance 2023 · Review of Financial Studies 2023
6 · Synthesis: The Kornis Protocol v2
Step 1 — Classify the regime (dial: macro gates + news scan + VIX structure). Step 2 — Classify the day (trending vs oscillating, first 30–60 min). Step 3 — Locate the structure (walls: biggest OI strikes on the chain tab; band extremes; anchored VWAP). Step 4 — Time with confluence (your reference guide's stack: oscillator divergence + location + trigger candle). Step 5 — Vehicle by Greeks (IV rank decides ITM vs ATM; DTE ≥ 2–3× expected hold; spread ≤ 5–8%). Step 6 — Exit by rule (scale into strength; time-stop; regime-stop).
