Audit v1.1 — the program's calls, replayed on real tape

Pulls the last ~8 sessions of 5-minute bars for every name on your board, walks candle by candle exactly the way TODAY sees them, fires the LEGACY rules (what ran through Sep 29) and the FIXED rules (engine.js) side by side, and grades every fire against its own plan: stop and T1 as the ticket wrote them, 12 candles, inside the session. Nothing here is a forecast — it is what the rules would have told you to do, and what the stock did next.
Loads your TODAY board automatically. Runs in this tab — about 1 to 3 minutes for a full board over 60 days. Leave the tab in front until it says Done.
What this replay cannot show. Two of the live defects don't exist on closed bars: the half-formed-candle fires (the page polled mid-candle and the relay served cached snapshots) and stale-feed fires. Both only made LEGACY worse live than it reads here — so LEGACY here is its best case. Entries are the decision-candle close (live, the print 15 s later is about the same). Results are in stock-move R (1 R = the plan's stop); an option at 0.70Δ with a real spread does a little worse than the stock on every trade, winners and losers alike.
