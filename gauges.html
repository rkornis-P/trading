Your Trade — live conditions—
Trend Force—
The math: every signal (VWAP stretch, 9EMA slope, MACD, RSI, multi-timeframe) is converted to a z-score — how many standard deviations from its own recent normal — so the gauge self-calibrates to each ticker's personality. The weighted sum passes through tanh (a saturation curve: no single signal can peg the needle) and is then multiplied by an ADX damper — in chop, the whole needle deflates toward zero, because trend readings inside chop are statistically meaningless. Right of center = bull force, left = bear force. Beyond ±60 = strong; a needle pinned past ±85 is stretched — late to chase.
Fuel—
The math: participation measured three ways — RVOL (today's cumulative volume ÷ the average at this exact time of day over prior sessions — the honest comparison), range expansion (current ATR vs its session baseline), and squeeze state (Bollinger inside Keltner = compressed energy). Trend Force says which way the car points; Fuel says whether there's gas. A big Trend reading on an empty Fuel tank is the classic trap — a drift, not a move. Below 35 = no trade regardless of direction.
Theta War — your position—
The math: from the live chain, your contract's delta and theta are computed via Black-Scholes (IV back-solved from the actual mid). Then the war, in dollars per hour per contract: what delta earns at the tape's current pace — Δ × (ATR-per-hour) — versus what theta charges to sit — |Θ| ÷ 6.5 trading hours. Needle green: movement is out-earning decay; the option pays rent. Needle red: you are paying by the hour to hold a hope. This gauge is why scalps have time stops — when the tape slows, the war flips even while price goes nowhere.

Reading the Panel — the nuances

The gauges answer three different questions. Trend Force: which way and how hard is the tape leaning? Fuel: is there real participation behind it? Theta War: is YOUR contract getting paid for sitting in it? A trade wants all three — direction, gas, and rent being earned.
TF strong + Fuel highThe real thing. Trend with participation — trail the 9EMA, let it work.
TF strong + Fuel <35The classic trap. A drift that looks like a trend — no witnesses, retraces easily. No entries; tighten if in.
TF fading toward 0 while in a tradeYour edge is evaporating in real time even if price holds. Take half or tighten — do not wait for red.
TF pinned ±85+Stretched. Late to enter, right to trail — snapback risk highest exactly when it looks best.
Fuel rising + squeeze releasedEnergy arriving. If TF agrees with your side, this is the add-on-strength moment (never past 2:00 CT).
Theta War red + TF flatPaying rent on a stalled tape — the invisible loss. This is a time-stop firing. Exit beats hope.
Theta War green + TF against youThe tape moves, but the wrong way for your side — earn rate means nothing if delta points backward. Trust TF's sign.
All three againstWrong trade or wrong day. Flat is a position.
Distance in ATRs beats distance in dollars. "$0.60 from the stop" means nothing by itself; "1.2 average candles from the stop" tells you it can hit in six minutes. The trade panel shows both — learn to think in candles.

Deltas between readings matter more than levels. A Trend Force of +40 falling from +65 is a worse sign than +30 rising from +10. The conditions line tracks this for you — direction of change is the earliest signal you get.

The panel never overrides the law. Bracket resting, 2:45 flat, 21 DTE, volume veto. The gauges are instruments; you are the pilot.
