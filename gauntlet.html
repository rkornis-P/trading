ATR — the stock's normal step size

ATR (Average True Range) measures how far a stock typically moves in one candle — its normal breathing, in dollars. On the 5-minute chart, the 5-minute ATR is the average full height (high to low, including any gap) of the last 14 five-minute candles. It is the single most important number for sizing a trade, because a dollar means something different on a $40 stock than on a $940 one — but one ATR always means the same thing: one normal move.

Why the Gauntlet is built on it. Every level you see was sized in ATRs so it fits the stock you're actually trading:

STOP = the 9EMA/VWAP shelf, minus a 0.15-ATR buffer. A stop closer than the noise gets hit by ordinary breathing; the ATR buffer keeps it just beyond.

T1 (first target) = trigger ± 2.2 ATR. About two normal moves — ambitious but reachable on a real push, not a fantasy.

TRAILING STOP (after T1) = 1.5 × ATR × delta. The leash must be longer than one breath or the wiggle stops you out.

SCORE includes an "it can travel" test (ATR ÷ price ≥ 0.18%) — the machine refuses to rank a stock that doesn't move enough to pay.

A full worked example — XOM, 5-minute ATR = $0.28:

That $0.28 means XOM has moved about 28¢ per 5-minute candle lately.

• A stop only 15¢ away sits inside the noise — you'd be stopped by random ticks. Bad.
• The Gauntlet's stop sits ~0.7 ATR below the shelf (~$0.42 of room) — beyond the breathing, so only a real reversal triggers it.
• T1 at 2.2 ATR = 2.2 × 0.28 ≈ $0.62 of stock travel from the trigger — reachable on one good push.
• The trailing stop = 1.5 × 0.28 × 0.70Δ ≈ $0.30 on the option — rides just outside XOM's normal wiggle.

Now compare SPCX with a $0.70 ATR: the very same formulas give a wider stop, a ~$1.54 T1 distance, and a $0.75 trail — 2½× everything — because SPCX breathes 2½× harder. Same rules, sized to the animal. That is why one fixed dollar stop can't work across stocks, and why the Gauntlet does the ATR math for you on every name.

Rule of thumb: stops beyond one ATR, targets a few ATRs out, trails about 1.5 ATR behind. Watch it change through the day — rising ATR means the stock is getting wild; size down.
